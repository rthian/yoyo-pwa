# AP Yo-Yo Open 2026 Competition Rules

> **Source:** [apyoyo.com/pages/judging-rules](https://apyoyo.com/pages/judging-rules)
> **Ruleset Code:** `AP_26`
> **Applies to:** AP Yo-Yo Open 2026 and events adopting this ruleset

---

## Introduction

- Competitors may choose to compete in one or more divisions.
- Select a song for your 1 Minute Prelim and 3 Minute Final Freestyle routine.
- Songs must be family-friendly, with no explicit language or profanities.
- The goal is to encourage more dynamic freestyles and simplify the rules to reduce uncertainty in judging criteria.
- This system will be reviewed annually and may be revised.

---

## Competition Format

### 1A Division

- All competitors (unless seeded) must compete in the 1 Minute Preliminaries.
- The **Top 5** qualifiers from Preliminaries advance directly to the 3 Minute Finals.
- Competitors placing **6th to 25th** in Preliminaries advance to the 1.5 Minute Semi-Finals.
- The **Top 10** Semi-Finalists then move on to the 3 Minute Finals.

**1A Finals consist of:**
- Seeded players
- Top 5 from Preliminaries
- Top 10 from Semi-Finals

### 2A / 3A / 4A / 5A Divisions

- All competitors (unless seeded) must compete in the 1 Minute Preliminaries.
- Top qualifiers progress to the 3 Minute Finals, joining the seeded players.
- A 1.5 Minute Semi-Final may be introduced if there is a high number of competitors.

### Round Structure Summary

| Round | Duration | Who Competes |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| **Prelim** | 1 minute | All non-seeded competitors |
| **Semi-Final** | 1.5 minutes | 6th-25th from Prelim (1A); optional for other divisions |
| **Final** | 3 minutes | Seeded players + Top 5 Prelim + Top 10 Semi (1A) |

### Seeding

- **1A Division National Champions** – Seeded to Semi-Finals
- **X Division National Champions** – Seeded to Finals
- **All AP Champions** (including 1A) – Seeded to Finals

## Judging System

### Score Split

| Component | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| **Technical Execution (TE)** | 50% |
| **Performance Evaluation (PE)** | 50% |

### Technical Execution (TE) – 50%

Judges use 2 clickers:

- **1st Clicker (Positive)** – Points given per unique trick element
- **2nd Clicker (Negative)** – Points deducted per trick or control miss

**Major deductions are absorbed into negative clicks:**
- Yo-yo rewind/restart: -1 click
- Yo-yo change/discard: -3 clicks

There is no separate Major Deductions category.

### Performance Evaluation (PE) – 50%

PE is divided into 5 equal components, each worth 20% of the PE score:

#### 1. Performance Quality (20%)
Stage presence, confidence, audience connection.

#### 2. Musicality (20%)
Synchronisation with music, rhythm, choreography.

#### 3. Trick Diversity (20%)
Range of tricks performed.

#### 4. Uniqueness (20%)
Originality, creativity, tricks or concepts not commonly seen.

#### 5. Execution (20%)
Smoothness, control, overall cleanliness.

### Final Score Formula

```
Final Score = Technical Execution (50%) + Performance Evaluation (50%)
```

No separate Major Deductions are applied. Deductions for yo-yo stops and changes are handled through negative TE clicks with equivalent weightage.

## Stage Rules

### Allowed Items on Stage
- Yo-yos
- Strings
- Counterweights
- Gloves (or equivalent items)
- Backup music device for competition
- Tray provided by contest officials

### Prohibited Items
- Maintenance tools
- Performance props
- Bags, cases, cloths, jackets, or extra clothing (other than what the contestant is already wearing)
- Any items not listed in the allowed section

## Key Differences from IYYF-WYYC-25

| Aspect | AP-26 | IYYF-WYYC-25 |
|--------|-------|---------------|
| **TE/PE Split** | 50% / 50% | 60% / 40% |
| **PE Categories** | 5 (each 20%) | 8 in Finals, 4 in Prelim/Semi |
| **Major Deductions** | None (absorbed into TE negative clicks) | Separate system (-1/-3/-5/DQ) |
| **PE Categories** | Performance Quality, Musicality, Trick Diversity, Uniqueness, Execution | EXE, CTL, TDV, SEM, MU1, MU2, BDY, SHW |
| **WildCard Round** | None | 30s, TE only |
| **Semi-Final Duration** | 1.5 minutes | 90 seconds |
| **TE Normalization** | Not specified | Yes, across judges |
