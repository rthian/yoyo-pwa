# IYYF WYYC2025 Contest Rules

> **Source:** [iyyf.org/wyyc2025-rules](https://iyyf.org/wyyc2025-rules/)
> **Ruleset Code:** `IYYF_WYYC_25`
> **Applies to:** World Yo-Yo Contest 2025 and events adopting this ruleset

---

## Championship Divisions

Yo-Yo has various play styles, and in competition there are 5 main divisions from 1A to 5A plus AP. They are separated depending on the number and type of Yo-Yo used. The winner of each division is awarded a World Champion title.

There are no class divisions by gender or age for Championship Divisions.

- **1A** – String Trick Freestyle with One Long-Spinning Yo-Yo
- **2A** – Looping Trick Freestyle with Two Responsive Yo-Yos
- **3A** – String Trick Freestyle with Two Long-Spinning Yo-Yos
- **4A** – Freestyle with Off String Yo-Yo(s)
- **5A** – Freestyle with Counter-Weight Yo-Yo(s)
- **AP** – Art & Performance Division

### Division Definitions

- **1A:** Freestyle with one string-trick yo-yo with the string attached to one hand. Tricks are based on mounts and touches between the yo-yo and string. (One Yo-Yo. One String. String must be attached to hand and yo-yo.)
- **2A:** Freestyle with two looping yo-yos with the strings attached to both hands. Tricks are based on making circles ("loops") with the yo-yos' trajectory. (Two Yo-Yos. Two Strings.)
- **3A:** Freestyle with two string-trick yo-yos with the strings attached to both hands. Tricks are based on mounts and touches between two yo-yos and two strings. (Two Yo-Yos. Two Strings.)
- **4A:** Freestyle with yo-yo(s) to which the string(s) is not attached. Tricks are based on moves possible because the yo-yo is not attached to the string. (One or more Yo-Yos. String attached to hand but not yo-yo.)
- **5A:** Freestyle with yo-yo(s) which has counter-weight(s) on the other side of string(s). Tricks are based on moves possible because the yo-yo has counter-weight and is not attached to the hand. (One or more Yo-Yos. String attached to yo-yo and weight instead of the hand.)

### Yo-Yos Allowed Simultaneously Per Division

- 1A – 1 yo-yo
- 2A – 2 yo-yos
- 3A – 2 yo-yos
- 4A – 1 or more yo-yos
- 5A – 1 or more yo-yos

## Non-Championship Exhibitions

### Women's Freestyle
- Only women can compete
- 2 minute freestyle (Final), 1 minute (Prelim)
- Choose one style from 1A-5A

### Over-40 Freestyle
- Only players 40 years old or older can compete
- 2 minute freestyle (Final), 1 minute (Prelim)
- Choose one style from 1A-5A
- Style normalization coefficients applied: 1A=1.00, 2A=1.28, 3A=1.35, 4A=1.19, 5A=1.27

### Junior Freestyle
- Only players 16 years old or younger can compete
- 90 second freestyle (Final), 30 second (Prelim, WildCard music list)
- Choose one style from 1A-5A

## Round Structure (1A-5A)

Players must advance through multiple rounds of competition to reach Finals. Seeded players from recognized National Contests and previous World Contests may begin from later rounds.

### Round 1: WildCard
- **Freestyle Length:** 30 seconds
- **Scoring:** Only Technical Execution (TE) -- clickers only
- **Music:** Choose from the Official WildCard Music List
- **Who Competes:** National Contest 11th place and under, did not compete in a National Contest, or anyone not already qualified to other rounds
- **Notes:** No Freestyle Evaluation, no Major Deductions. Private room with judges and staff only.

### Round 2: Prelim
- **Freestyle Length:** 1 minute
- **Scoring:** Technical Execution (TE), 4 Freestyle Evaluations (FE), and Major Deductions (MD)
- **Music:** Contestant's choice
- **Who Competes:** WildCard Winners, National Contest 2nd to 10th place, and 2024 World Finalists 4th place and under

### Round 3: Semi-Final
- **Freestyle Length:** 90 seconds
- **Scoring:** Technical Execution (TE), 4 Freestyle Evaluations (FE), and Major Deductions (MD)
- **Music:** Contestant's choice
- **Who Competes:** Prelim Winners, National Champions, and 2024 World 2nd & 3rd place

### Round 4: Final
- **Freestyle Length:** 3 minutes
- **Scoring:** Technical Execution (TE), 8 Freestyle Evaluations (FE), and Major Deductions (MD)
- **Music:** Contestant's choice
- **Who Competes:** Semi-Final Winners and 2024 World Champions

### Seed Sliding Rule for WYYC Top 3
If one of the WYYC top 3 takes their own national title, their redundant seed from nationals goes to the next best player. If the WYYC top 3 take all top 3 places of their national contest, 4th place gets the seed.

### Dual Citizenship / Residency
- A contestant can only compete in one National Contest per year
- Must have citizenship or permanent residency in that country
- Only one chance to acquire a seed spot through National Contests per World Contest

## Scoring System

Freestyles are graded on two criteria: **Technical Execution (TE)** and **Freestyle Evaluation (FE)**.

Judges are assigned into two groups:
- **Group A:** Technical Execution (TE)
- **Group B:** Freestyle Evaluation (FE)

### Technical Execution (TE) – 60%

Each judge uses two clickers, one for positive and one for negative points. These are added together for the final TE score, which is 60% of the total possible score.

**Judge considers only:** success, difficulty, risk, and variation of each trick performed.

**NOT scored in TE:** Originality, amplitude, long-sleep, continuity, uniqueness, style, choreography.

#### Positive Points
Points are given per trick element when the contestant performs advanced level trick elements.

**1A Examples:** Mount (Trapeze), Hop (Eli Hop), Laceration (Hook), Whip Catch (Iron Whip, Slack Trapeze)

**2A Examples:** Looping (Loop, Hop), Moon (Reach for the Moon), Wrap, Tangler, Around the World

**3A Examples:** Rolls (Velvet Rolls), Kink (Kink Fu), Trapeze (2-Hand), Assisted, KoroKoro

**4A Examples:** Whip (Over Whip), Recapture, Boingy Boingy, Toss, Orbit (Around the Arm)

**5A Examples:** Direction Change (Shoulder Pop), 360, Propeller, Bee-sting, Aerial (Meltdown Jump)

Generally, the same trick elements performed in a freestyle will not be scored the second time. High-risk repeating tricks (e.g., Suicide combo) or same elements in different combos can be scored again with some reduction.

#### Negative Points
- **Negative 1:** Trapeze miss, control miss, catch miss, corkscrew in looping
- All deductions are counted per yo-yo
- Yo-Yo stop or change counted separately as Major Deductions

### Freestyle Evaluation (FE) – 40%

Judges (Group B) evaluate categories from 0 to 10 points. Total points are halved to make 40% of the final score. Scores are NOT normalized between judges.

#### Final Round: 8 Categories (total 80 points, halved to 40)

1. **Execution (EXE)** – Success rate of tricks, completion, fewer mistakes
2. **Control (CTL)** – Control of yo-yo/string, smooth landings, flowing transitions
3. **Trick Diversity (TDV)** – Trick choice, mix, order, and presentation variety
4. **Space Use & Emphasis (SEM)** – Amplitude, stage use, focusing on subject effectively
5. **Music Use 1: Choreography (MU1)** – Hitting sounds, beats, tempo, rhythm, cues
6. **Music Use 2: Construction (MU2)** – Mood, imagery, tone, theme, routine construction
7. **Body Control (BDY)** – Stage manners, posture, professionalism, attitude
8. **Showmanship (SHW)** – Theme/story, enjoyment, entertainment, overall impression

#### Prelim & Semi-Final: 4 Categories (total 40 points, halved to 20, then scaled to 40)

1. **Execution (EXE)**
2. **Control (CTL)**
3. **Choreography (MU1)**
4. **Body Control (BDY)**

### Major Deductions (MD)

Subtracted after all scores above are summed. All deductions are per yo-yo/string.

| Deduction | Points | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Yo-Yo Stop (Restart)** | -1 | Any stop of yo-yo spin with string fully unwound, or needing to help the yo-yo regain spin |
| **Yo-Yo Discard (Change)** | -3 | Any yo-yo discard, loss, change, or stopping use of the yo-yo |
| **Yo-Yo Detach (String Cut)** | -5 | Yo-yo coming apart, string cut, or string detach |
| **Flying Off** | DQ | Any yo-yo jumping into audience area behind judges' table |

**Performance End Rules:**
- If contestant cannot fully wind yo-yo at performance end, both a stop and discard are counted as a single discard (-3)
- If detach happens at end, only detach (-5) is counted (not additional discard)

### Final Score Formula

```
Final Score = Technical Execution (max 60.00) + Freestyle Evaluation (max 40.00) - Major Deductions
```

- TE is normalized across judges (to eliminate unevenness)
- FE is NOT normalized
- When more than 5 judges per category, highest and lowest scores may be removed

### Tie-Breaking
Judges determine the winner using descending order of TE and FE. If still tied, judges call it a tie or suggest a solution. The judges' decision is final.

## Freestyle Regulations

### Performance Time
- **Final:** 3 minutes
- **Semi-Final:** 90 seconds
- **Prelim:** 1 minute
- **WildCard:** 30 seconds
- Music starts the timer; music is stopped between 3:00 and 3:01

### Music Rules
- Contestant's choice of music (except WildCard which uses official list)
- Music must be family-friendly, suitable for all ages
- No explicit language, profanity, violence, sexual content, or discrimination
- Music must be legally obtained, high-quality files
- Low quality rips (e.g., YouTube to MP3) discouraged

### Performing Area
- Stage box: 6 meters wide x 4 meters depth (when available)
- No penalty for going outside the box, but judges may not see tricks

### Stage Rules
- Performance must start with yo-yo fully wound in hand
- No yo-yoing before music starts
- Cannot leave the stage during freestyle
- Only the contestant allowed on stage (no assistants)
- Sitting or standing over stage edge prohibited

### Allowed Items on Stage
- Yo-yos, strings, counter-weights, gloves (or equivalent)
- Backup music device
- Tray provided by contest officials
- **Prohibited:** Bags, cases, cloth, jackets, extra clothing, tables, performance props, maintenance tools

### Code of Conduct
- Unsafe performance may result in disqualification
- Inappropriate behavior or speech on stage may result in disqualification
- Disturbing other contestants is strictly prohibited

## Art & Performance Division (AP)

AP is the division where a player or team is judged predominantly on performance rather than technical skill. Choreography, use of music, creative use of the stage, movement, and performance are the key judging criteria.

### Awards
- **Grand-Prix Award** – World Champion Title (may not be awarded every year)
- **Entertainment Award** – Power to delight and entertain
- **Artistic Award** – Beauty of expression, high level of artistry
- **Creativity Award** – Originality, uniqueness, freshness

### AP Rules
- Performances up to 4 minutes
- Video submission prelim required (deadline: June 30)
- 3-9 players/teams selected for Finals from submitted videos
- Teams are allowed; assistants are team members
- Yo-yo must be the main focus of the performance
- No deduction for mistakes, but zero/near-zero mistakes expected
- Props allowed with advance approval (no fire, pyrotechnics, or knives)
- Props larger than 1m x 1m x 1m require judge approval
- Setup time max 2 minutes without prior approval
- Lighting: choose normal or dark (for light-up yo-yos/projectors)
- Previous year's AP World Champion seeded to Finals
- Prelim video must match actual stage performance

## Definition of Competitive Yo-Yoing

- All tricks/moves must utilize both yo-yo(s) and string(s) to receive points
- Yo-yo(s) must return to the contestant's hand with fully wound string(s) with its own spin power
- All tricks must be performed while the yo-yo(s) is spinning
- Yo-yo(s) must start spinning with the power coming from the unwinding string
